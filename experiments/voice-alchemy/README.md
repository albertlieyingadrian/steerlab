# Which unit of a model composes most predictably? (Voice Alchemy)

Research note: [`report/index.html`](report/index.html) ([live](https://albertlieyingadrian.github.io/steerlab/experiments/voice-alchemy/report/)).

Push one unit of a language model on every word and its writing voice changes; push two at once and you get a mix.
This study measures, for six kinds of unit, whether the mix is the sum of its parts:
- **neuron communities** (groups of MLP neurons that fire together), the main unit;
- **single MLP neurons**, as in Neuron Alchemist;
- **SAE features** (Gemma Scope; Qwen-Scope for Qwen);
- **circuits** (transcoder features from circuit-tracer attribution graphs);
- two baselines: a **difference-of-means direction** and **prompting**.

Units are labelled by a **measured personality profile** (eight voices, five strengths), not a one-word name. The main
model is Qwen3-8B; Gemma-2-2B-it, where the study was pre-registered and first run, is the cross-model check under the
same judge. The plan, with its design, predictions and every dated amendment, was fixed before any confirmatory run.

Inspired by [Neuron Alchemist](https://alchemist.christianyun.com/) and [neuronary.org](https://neuronary.org).

## Results (16 held-out prompts, 9,808 judged replies per model)

| Unit | Qwen3-8B: moves its voice | Qwen: scorable pairs, median E | Gemma-2-2B-it: moves its voice | Gemma: scorable pairs, median E |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt (baseline) | 8/8 | 28, 0.33 | 8/8 | 28, 0.33 |
| Direction (baseline) | **7/8** | **21, 0.41 [0.37, 0.45]** | **8/8** | **28, 0.30 [0.26, 0.37]** |
| SAE feature | 5/8 | 10, 0.50 | 5/8 | 10, 0.43 |
| Single neuron | 1/8 | 0 | 4/8 | 6, 0.52 |
| Neuron community | 3/8 | 3, 0.33 | 2/8 | 1, 0.32 |
| Circuit | 0/8 | 0 | 0/8 | 0 |

E is the additivity error (0 = the pair is exactly the sum of its parts). In both models 16 of 28 direction pairs break
at full strength, and the cosine between two pushes does not predict E (Spearman 0.03 Qwen, -0.02 Gemma). Random units
of the same shape move their voice by about 0.1 points on average.

## Files

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `va.py` | Voices, prompt sets, loop measure, composition score and label, spend ledger, per-model settings (`VA_MODEL=gemma\|qwen`). |
| `smoke_modal.py` | The TransformerLens feasibility check on Modal. |
| `gpu.py` | TransformerLens work: set-F replies, activations, every unit and its random twin, batched steered generation. |
| `modal_app.py` | Runs `gpu.py` stages on Modal H100s (`--stage find` / `--stage gen`). |
| `circuits.py` | circuit-tracer attribution graphs and the circuit units (its own image). |
| `judge.py` | The judge (one call per reply and aspect), the known-answer ruler, API and Modal (open-model) providers. |
| `open_judge.py` | Open-weight judges on Modal with vLLM; `open_judge_qwen32b_check.py` is the one-off Qwen3-32B label check. |
| `pipeline.py` | Job building per round, scoring, selection of units and strengths, the circuit-pair rule. |
| `analyze.py` | Profiles, polysemanticity, composition labels, geometry, the type comparison and the predictions. |
| `recheck.py` | Second-family re-check on the held-out outputs. |
| `build_table.py`, `report/alchemy.js` | The precomputed alchemy table (`report/table_qwen.js`, `report/table_gemma.js`; `VA_MODEL=qwen` builds Qwen's). |
| `make_figures.py`, `make_cover.py`, `record_demo.py` | Figures, cover and thumbnail, and the demo recording. |
| `reproduce.sh` | Every step in order (`--dry-run`, `--scores`). |
| `results.tsv` | The development ledger (every tuning step, its metric, cost and whether it was kept). |

## Data

- `data/known_answer.json`: 56 known-answer texts for the judge (`id`, `kind`, `traits`, `q`, `text`).
- `data/jobs_<round>.jsonl`: every generation job (`rid`, `set`, `kind`, `uids`, `traits`, `s`, `q`, `cat`, `pi`, `prefix`, `push`).
- `results/scored_<round>.jsonl`: every reply with `text`, `loops`, `repeat` and `scores[judge][aspect]`.
- `results/analysis_heldout.json`: every reported number; `results/selection.json`: chosen unit and strength per kind and voice.
- `results/qwen3-8b/`: every Qwen3-8B file (same names as Gemma's in `results/`). `units_meta.json`, `circuits.json`: the units.
- `results/ruler.json` (every judge tried), `results/*/recheck.json`, `results/recheck_qwen.json`: judge checks.
- Raw generations, unit vectors (`*.pt`) and the judge cache are regenerated by `reproduce.sh` and not committed.
