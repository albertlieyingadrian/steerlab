L1: # Bag fees
L2: All published bag fees apply at each check-in location and are base rates according to travel dates and destination; applicable taxes are not shown.
L4: ## Carry-on bags
L5: You're allowed 1 carry-on bag and 1 personal item in all cabins.
L16: ### Carry-on items
L17: You can bring only one carry-on item along with your personal item. Your carry-on must fit in in the overhead bin or under the seat in front of you. If it doesn't fit, it will need to be checked. Be sure all your carry-on items are labeled in case they are checked.
L19: #### Carry-on bag
L20: The total size of your carry-on, including the handles and wheels, cannot exceed 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 36 x 23 cm) and must fit in the sizer at the airport.
L25: ## Checked bags
L26: Bag fees have been updated effective for tickets issued on / after February 20, 2024.
L28: Travel within / between the U.S., Puerto Rico, and U.S. Virgin Islands – 1st checked bag fee is $40 and the 2nd checked bag fee is $45.
L30: Travel to / from Canada, Caribbean, Mexico, Central America, and Guyana – 1st checked bag fee is $35 and the 2nd checked bag fee is $45.
L32: All bag fees are non-refundable and apply per person, each way, even if you buy or get an upgrade that includes free checked bags. If you believe you were incorrectly charged for bag fees, contact an airline representative for help or file a refund claim within 45 days.
