Research

Notes on steering open language models while they write: what we tried, what happened, and how sure we are. Every result is measured against a random direction of the same size, and every number can be rerun.

  1. Experiment

    Steering past an English-only prompt

    A system prompt says “English only” and holds against 24 tricks; a Chinese direction switches the reply anyway on four models, where random directions never do.

    Albert Lie · October 27, 2026

    Turning open models into Golden Gate fans

    A simple direction makes Gemma bring up the Golden Gate Bridge in 14 of 15 answers; Llama-3.1-8B needed a direction and a clamped SAE feature together.

    Albert Lie · October 27, 2026
