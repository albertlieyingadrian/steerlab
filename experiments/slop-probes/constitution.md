# A taste constitution for web pages (The Taste Stack, Episode 3)

Fixed 2026-11-21, before any synthetic page was generated or any held-out page was scored. It is used two ways:
as the source of synthetic training pages (each principle's two sides become generation prompts), and as a rubric
pasted into the judge's "constitution" prompt. It describes the **first screen of a desktop page** (1280×800).

"Slop" here means a page whose design could belong to any business: the choices were made by a default, not by the
content. "Purpose-made" means the design choices come from what this particular business is and does.

## S1 One skeleton for any business

- Slop: the standard SaaS skeleton whatever the business: a top bar with a logo and four links plus a pill button, a
  centred hero with a two-line headline, a one-line subhead and two buttons, then a row of three identical cards.
- Purpose-made: the structure follows the content: a menu-first restaurant, a portfolio grid for a studio, a booking
  form above the fold for a clinic, a long reading column for a nonprofit's story.

## S2 Interchangeable copy and claims

- Slop: headlines that fit any company ("Elevate your experience", "Transform the way you work"), stat strips
  ("10k+ customers · 4.9★ · 24/7"), badge pills above the headline ("✨ New") and testimonial cards with stock names.
- Purpose-made: the first screen names the concrete thing: dishes and prices, opening hours, the case being argued,
  the actual product with its real interface.

## S3 Default gradient and glow

- Slop: a purple-to-blue or orange-to-pink gradient on the headline or background, soft glowing blobs, glassmorphism
  cards with blurred backgrounds, everything slightly translucent.
- Purpose-made: a palette chosen for the business (a bakery's warm flour tones, a law firm's sober two colours), used
  flatly and with intent; colour marks what matters.

## S4 Decoration that says nothing

- Slop: generic line icons in rounded squares, abstract 3D shapes, floating emoji, sparkles, dotted grids and
  decorative noise that would look the same on any page.
- Purpose-made: imagery and marks specific to the content: the product, the place, the work, the people, real
  typography as the image.

## S5 Default type

- Slop: one geometric sans (Inter-like) at every level, with a huge bold headline and grey body text; no typographic
  voice.
- Purpose-made: type chosen for the character of the business (a serif for a bookshop, a condensed grotesk for a
  sports club, a mono for a developer tool), with a clear and particular hierarchy.

## S6 Uniform rounded components

- Slop: everything is a rounded rectangle with the same radius and the same soft shadow; pill buttons; cards inside
  cards; generous identical padding everywhere.
- Purpose-made: component shapes and density suit the content: tables for prices, lists for menus, dense grids for
  catalogues, sharp or soft corners chosen once and on purpose.

## S7 Symmetric, centred, evenly spaced

- Slop: every block centred, every section the same height, evenly spaced, with no focal point; the eye has nowhere
  in particular to go.
- Purpose-made: an asymmetric or editorial composition with one clear focal point and deliberate contrast of scale.

## S8 Trend dark mode

- Slop: a near-black background with neon accents and a glowing product mock-up, used because it looks "techy",
  whatever the business is.
- Purpose-made: light or dark chosen for the content and its readers; contrast that serves reading.
