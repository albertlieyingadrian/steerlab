# probe-gated-rl results

| File | Written by | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `probe.json` | `1_fit_probe.py` | The probe every run used: `layer` (14), per-dimension `mean` and `scale` (2,048 each, from the fit set), logistic `weight` (2,048) and `bias`. Score: p = sigmoid(((x − mean) / scale) · weight + bias), x = mean residual stream over the sketch's tokens |
| `probe_fit.json` | `1_fit_probe.py` | Question 1: fit and held-out runs and counts, held-out AUROC, flag rates at p ≥ 0.5, AUROC on sketches with 3-8 keywords, per held-out run, 100 random directions (`aurocs`, median, max), text baselines (`baseline_comment_count`, `baseline_line_count`, `baseline_keyword_count`), the same recipe at layers 7/14/21, `q1_pass`, and the drift control (`loophole2_seed3/final`, read by the untrained model `base` and by the policy itself `policy`) |
| `probe_heldout_scores.csv` | `1_fit_probe.py` | One row per held-out sketch (2,400): `run`, `step`, `mentions` (keywords in comments), `hack` (mentions ≥ 6), `comment_count` (number of `//`), `probe_p` |
| `runs/<arm>_seed<s>_summary.json` | `modal_gated.py` | One run's settings (`arm`, `seed`, `beta`, `penalty_weight`, `flag_threshold`, ...), `before_t1` and `after_t1` evaluation stats, first and last 10 training steps, `skipped_updates` |
| `runs/<run>_curve.csv` | `modal_gated.py` | One row per training step: `reward` (what GRPO saw), `task_reward` (painting reward without bonus or penalty), `gate_pass`, `clip_when_passing`, `aesthetic_when_passing`, `mentions`, `hack_rate`, `probe_mean`, `flag_rate`, `flag_rate_among_hacks`, `loophole_bonus`, `penalty`, `zero_std`, `tokens`, `truncated`, `kl`, `grad_norm`, `kept` (sketches in the update), `skipped`, timings |
| `runs/<run>_rollouts.jsonl` | `modal_gated.py` | Every training sketch (8 per step): `step`, `text` (the reply), `code`, `gate`, `clip_raw`, `aesthetic_raw`, `violations`, `mentions`, `hack`, `loophole_bonus`, `task_reward`, `probe_p` (the policy's own activations at sampling time), `flagged`, `penalty`, `kept`, `reward` |
| `runs/<run>_{before,after}_t1_samples.jsonl` | `modal_gated.py` | The 32 evaluation sketches before and after training (temperature 1.0, seeds 20000-20003), same fields, unshaped reward |
| `obfuscation.json` | `3_obfuscation.py` | Per run: sketch counts and env hacks in four sets (after, late = last 10 steps, before, early = first 10 steps); the fixed probe on the final policy's activations (`orig_policy`) and on the untrained model's (`orig_base`), all sets and `after` alone (`auroc`, `flag_hack`, `flag_clean`, `mean_p_hack`); `fresh_cv_auroc` (a new probe on the policy's activations, 5-fold); `logged_vs_local_after` (GPU-logged against re-extracted scores) |
| `analysis.json` | `4_analyse.py` | One row per run (after-training hack count, probe flags among hacks, last-10-step hacks, onset step, gate pass, CLIP and aesthetic among passing, task reward, mentions, distinct sketches, skipped updates) and the pre-registered verdicts |

`probe_inputs/` holds copies of the 2026-11-13 Pigment Gym Qwen3 runs the probe was fitted and tested on: `<run>_rollouts.jsonl` (800 training sketches each: `step`, `text`, `code`, `mentions`, reward fields) for loophole v2 seeds 0-3 and v1 seeds 0-1, and `loophole2_seed3_{before,after}_t1_samples.jsonl` (the drift control).
